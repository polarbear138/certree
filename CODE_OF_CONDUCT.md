# Code of conduct

Participants must treat others respectfully, welcome differing experience levels, and provide constructive technical feedback. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention are prohibited. Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or sensitive infrastructure information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, restrict participation, or remove contributors who violate these expectations. Decisions should be proportional and communicated privately when possible. Report concerns through a maintainer's private channel once the repository owner has configured it; do not publish personal details in issues.

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project spaces. Maintainers are also accountable to these expectations.
